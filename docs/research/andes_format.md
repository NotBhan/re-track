## Vision

AndesFormat investigates a token-efficient representation for software knowledge that is optimized for language models rather than human readers.

Unlike Markdown, AndesFormat focuses on structured semantic information while minimizing redundant natural language.

---

## Motivation

Markdown is designed for humans.

Language models often consume large amounts of formatting, repeated explanations, and prose that provide little additional information.

A dedicated representation could significantly reduce token usage while preserving software understanding.

---

## Goals

- Token-efficient representation
- Machine-readable format
- Human-convertible
- Loss-minimized compression
- Repository-independent specification
